02

The service

CourseSnag helps users view public Cornell Class Roster information and track selected course sections.

  • Local mode checks from your browser and requires the tab and computer to remain active.
  • Cloud mode stores a Discord-linked watchlist, checks it while cloud monitoring is active, and sends qualifying status alerts by Discord direct message.

Cloud mode may be placed into seasonal standby outside course-planning periods. Features may be changed, limited, interrupted, or discontinued at any time.

03

Accounts and eligibility

You may use Local mode without an account. Cloud mode requires a permitted Discord account, a server where you can add the CourseSnag bot, and direct messages that the bot can deliver. CourseSnag requests no server permissions. You are responsible for activity through your browser session and for keeping your devices and Discord account secure.

Do not connect another person’s Discord account or track courses on another person’s behalf without permission.
